# Design System: Naixu Guo

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One researcher, three atmospheres"**

Light (`ink`, default) and Dark (`black`) share one watercolor composition, EB Garamond lettering, restrained Hanken prose, aligned navigation and reading pages. White uses cool pale paper and navy ink; Black uses near-black blue paper, light ink and a graphite/silver dragon. The dragon and exact right-side poem belong only to the homepage hero. The lower homepage and inner pages use quiet related paper, with live HTML text and controls.

Game (`dark`) retains the Persona 5-inspired red, black, cutout lettering and grayscale portrait. P3 (`light`) was removed at the author’s request on 2026-11-04; its stylesheet is archived locally and never loaded. Classic remains retired. Both removed preferences resolve to Light. Names shown to visitors are simple and familiar: Light, Dark and Game.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White and Black are palette/artwork siblings with the same layout and typography.
- Shared dragon-theme shells and page heads align Research, Publications, Library and CV.
- Three-step explanatory research sequences pair text-free SVG drawings with short HTML captions; original scientific figures remain linked and Publications owns the full paper record.
- Game preserves the graphic appearance; removed P3 preferences fall back to Light.
